# Superseded: Master Requirements (docx)

`Calais_Open_Commons_Protocol_Master_Requirements.docx` is **historical** and **superseded**.

Do not treat it as the active source of truth. Prefer `requirements.md` and the Model B settlement rules below.

## Active Model B (consistent with requirements.md Financial rule; supersedes the docx mainnet router)

- Sponsors pay in **EUR** (card/SEPA).
- After the refund window, **90%** settles as **USDC on Base** to verified associations through an **authorised CASP**.
- Up to **10%** admin, in **EUR**, **capped at documented costs**; any unused remainder goes to named local charities, so 100% of net profit is donated and reported publicly.
- Settlement rehearsal and contracts are **Base testnet only**. There is **no mainnet router** in this repository; mainnet remains blocked behind launch gates and explicit owner confirmation.

The legacy docx describes founder-supplied `adminWallet` auto-split, and “Deploy non-upgradeable mainnet router”. Those statements are obsolete under Model B.
