## Active Model B (supersedes docx “100% net profit” / mainnet router)

- Sponsors pay in **EUR** (card/SEPA).
- After the refund window, **90%** settles as **USDC on Base** to verified associations through an **authorised CASP**.
- **10%** admin stays in **EUR**.
- Settlement rehearsal and contracts are **Base testnet only**. There is **no mainnet router** in this repository; mainnet remains blocked behind launch gates and explicit owner confirmation.

The legacy docx still describes “100% of net profit”, founder-supplied `adminWallet` auto-split, and “Deploy non-upgradeable mainnet router”. Those statements are obsolete under Model B.
